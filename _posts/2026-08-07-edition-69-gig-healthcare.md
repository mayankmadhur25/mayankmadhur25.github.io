No, not the problem people think it fixes. Gig platforms solve scheduling; India's workforce crisis is distribution and financing. It is a capacity instrument, not an equity instrument. And underneath sits an accountability problem nobody has written down: when a home nurse takes instructions from a platform, a hospital and a family, who is responsible when something goes wrong?
