NFHS-6: 95.9% of pregnant women see a health worker once, 65.2% complete four check-ups, 37.8% finish their iron tablets, and 15.3% of toddlers eat an adequate diet. These are separate indicators, not one cohort, but together they show one pattern: India solved arrival and still measures arrival. Three programmes launched in eight weeks in 2026 try to fix completion, and three of the nine steps still publish no number at all.
