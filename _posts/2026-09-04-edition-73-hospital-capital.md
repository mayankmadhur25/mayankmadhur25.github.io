In Q1 FY27, hospitals under Kotak's coverage grew revenue 21% and EBITDA 22% while operational beds rose 15% and margins stayed flat near 23%. ICRA expects roughly 14,500 more beds at a capex of Rs 30,000-32,000 crore across FY26 and FY27. So the question is no longer who owns the beds, it is how fast each one becomes productive. I re-tested the popular claim that occupancy drives margin using CRISIL peer data: across the same six chains the correlation flips from -0.30 in FY25 to +0.30 in FY26, with confidence intervals spanning zero. A one-year cross-section cannot identify the operating lever. And KKR's $1.4bn Medicover deal is less of a turnaround than it looks. Management says 19 of 25 hospitals are already profitable and that a rising debt position was what prompted the sale.
