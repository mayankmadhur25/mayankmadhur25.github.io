Price tells you what one unit costs. It says nothing about what that unit achieves, for whom, or over what period. Healthcare has at least six different ways of asking "is this worth it?", and they give different answers to the same question. This edition works through a treatment comparison where the price gap is not the decision, offers an interactive model where changing one assumption flips the conclusion, and sets out four traps in language that sound similar but are not: cost saving, cost effective, cost minimising and affordable. India already has the machinery for this in HTAIn under the Department of Health Research, which makes it a question of use rather than of building something new.
