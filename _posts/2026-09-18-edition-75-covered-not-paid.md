India insured 60.2% of its households, up 19 points in three years. Insurers paid 71 paise on every rupee claimed. IRDAI reports a settlement rate of 87.5% by claim count, but run the same table by rupee value and it falls to 71.1%: of Rs 1,32,488 crore claimed in FY25, Rs 29,933 crore never arrived. Most of that, Rs 18,521 crore, was not rejection at all. It was disallowance, money trimmed off bills insurers had already agreed to settle, and it carries a claim count of zero. Meanwhile claims ratios span 41 points across firms selling the same product, government schemes cover 42% of lives for 8% of premium, and coverage rose while the public share of institutional births fell and caesareans reached 54.1% in private facilities.
