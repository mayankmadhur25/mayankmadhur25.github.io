In India's public hospitals and health centres, patients now register online and doctors enter most consultations. The digital record thins at the pharmacy, inside bigger hospitals and on the next visit. Evidence from Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Delhi, Bihar and 32 more states and UTs, with six measures that would show what works.
