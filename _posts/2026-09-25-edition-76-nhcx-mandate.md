NHA's dashboard shows 46,996 government-scheme hospitals on NHCX and 867 private ones. Everyone is waiting for a notification that fills the private lane. It is not coming. Three other doors are already opening: the DPDP deadline in May 2027, a medical education regulator that has started punishing colleges, and an insurer that has to approve claims within one hour.
