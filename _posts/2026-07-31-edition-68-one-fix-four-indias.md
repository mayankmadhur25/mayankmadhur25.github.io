The insurance debate argues about how much cover to add. Wrong axis. India runs four health systems under one name, each breaking at a different point in the same six-step chain, so no single national fix reaches all four. One comes closer than the rest: generic medicines by molecule name. It is the cheapest lever available, and nothing else reaches an affluent household and a scheme-covered one on the same day.
